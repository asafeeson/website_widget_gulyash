Neon Genesis Evangelion

About the Series

Neon Genesis Evangelion is a Japanese anime series created by Hideaki Anno. It first aired in 1995 and is considered one of the most influential anime of all time. The story follows Shinji Ikari, a teenager recruited by the mysterious organization NERV to pilot a giant bio-machine mecha called an Evangelion to protect Earth from mysterious beings known as Angels.

Key Themes

  • Psychological exploration of characters
  • Existentialism and philosophy
  • Human relationships and trauma
